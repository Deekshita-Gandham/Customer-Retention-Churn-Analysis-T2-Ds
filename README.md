# Customer Retention & Churn Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes customer churn and retention patterns using a telecom customer dataset.

The objective is to identify:

- Why customers leave the platform
- Which customer segments are most likely to churn
- Customer lifetime trends
- Retention drivers
- Business recommendations to reduce churn

---

## Business Problem

Customer churn directly impacts company revenue and growth.

The goal of this analysis is to discover patterns behind customer attrition and provide actionable recommendations to improve customer retention.

---

## Dataset

Dataset: Telco Customer Churn Dataset

Features include:

- Gender

## Analysis Performed

### Churn by Contract Type

Month-to-month customers showed the highest churn rates.

### Churn by Tenure Group

New customers were significantly more likely to leave.

### Churn by Payment Method

Electronic check users experienced higher churn.

### Churn by Internet Service

Customer retention varied across service types.

### Churn by Tech Support

Customers without technical support churned more frequently.

---

## Key Findings

1. Month-to-month contracts have the highest churn risk.

2. Customers in their first year are most likely to leave.

3. Lack of technical support correlates with higher churn.

4. Long-term customers generate significantly higher lifetime value.

---

## Recommendations

### Improve Customer Onboarding

Focus on the first 12 months of the customer journey.

### Promote Long-Term Contracts

Provide incentives for yearly and multi-year subscriptions.

### Enhance Support Services

Encourage adoption of technical support services.

### Retention Campaigns

Target high-risk customers with proactive engagement.
